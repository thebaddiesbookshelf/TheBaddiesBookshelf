Before You Join

♡
Do I have to read the Book of the Month?

No. Book club selections are optional. Join whichever reads interest you and skip anything that does not.

Can I DNF books?

Absolutely. Your reading time belongs to you. You never have to finish a book you are not enjoying.

Do I have to participate every day?

No. Participate daily, occasionally, or whenever your schedule allows. There are no activity requirements.

Can I join and mostly lurk?

Yes. You are welcome to quietly explore until you feel comfortable joining conversations or activities.

Do audiobooks, Kindle books, and graphic novels count?

Yes. All reading formats count and are respected inside the community.

Can authors and writers join?

Yes. Readers, writers, indie authors, aspiring authors, reviewers, and bookish creatives are welcome.
