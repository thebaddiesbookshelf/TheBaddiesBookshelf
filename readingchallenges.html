Challenge Guidelines

♡
Do audiobooks and graphic novels count?

Yes. Audiobooks, graphic novels, manga, e-books, physical books, and other reading formats all count.

Can one book count for multiple prompts?

Unless a specific challenge says otherwise, you may decide whether to reuse books. We encourage unique titles when possible, but your reading experience should remain enjoyable.

Do I have to complete every prompt?

No. Complete as many prompts as you want. Challenges are designed to motivate you, not create pressure.

Where do I submit my progress?

Post your progress inside the designated challenge or tracking channel in The Baddies Bookshelf Discord.

How do I receive badges?

Follow the submission instructions for the challenge. Once your completion is confirmed, eligible badges or roles will be awarded.
