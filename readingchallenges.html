Challenge Guidelines

♡
Do audiobooks and graphic novels count?

Yes! Physical books, e-books, audiobooks, manga, and graphic novels all count unless a specific challenge says otherwise.

Can one book count for multiple prompts?

Unless a specific challenge says otherwise, you may decide whether to reuse books. We encourage unique titles when possible, but your reading experience should remain enjoyable.

Do I have to complete every challenge?

Not at all. Pick the challenges that sound fun to you and participate at your own pace. You can take on one, a few, or try all of them.

Where do I submit my progress?

Post your progress inside the designated challenge or tracking channel in The Baddies Bookshelf Discord.

How do I earn badges?

Badges are earned by completing their individual requirements. Some recognize reading milestones, while others are tied to events, challenges, community participation, and special achievements.
