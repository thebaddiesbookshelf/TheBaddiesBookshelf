How It Started

We Built the Bookish Space We Wanted to Join.

The Baddies Bookshelf started with one simple idea: readers should never have to defend what they love. Whether your shelf is filled with dark romance, fantasy, thrillers, urban fiction, cozy reads, or a little bit of everything, you deserve a community where your taste is celebrated.

What began as a place to talk about books quickly became something much bigger. It became a space for real friendships, themed events, reading challenges, movie nights, honest reviews, wild reactions, and the kind of bookish conversations that keep going long after the final chapter.

We are not here to tell you how to read. We are here to remind you that you already belong.

Frequently Asked Questions

✧
Is there a fee to join The Baddies Bookshelf?

No. Our main community spaces, reading discussions, and standard activities are free to join. Some optional events, exchanges, or special experiences may have separate costs, but those will always be clearly stated.

What genres does TBB focus on?

All genres are welcome. Our members especially enjoy romance, dark romance, thrillers, fantasy, urban fiction, romantasy, mystery, contemporary fiction, and cozy reads.

Do I have to participate every day?

Not at all. You can chat daily, join occasional events, participate in challenges, or quietly browse until you feel comfortable. There are no activity quotas.

Do audiobooks and e-books count?

Absolutely. Audiobooks, e-books, physical books, graphic novels, manga, and every other reading format count.

How do I join the Discord community?

Visit the Join Us page or use any Join Discord button on the website. Once inside, read the welcome information, select your roles, and introduce yourself whenever you are ready.
